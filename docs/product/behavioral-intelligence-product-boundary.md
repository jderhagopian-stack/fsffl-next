# Behavioral Intelligence product boundary

Behavioral Intelligence is a first-class, read-only evidence surface.

It answers: **What has this owner actually done in the available source-backed history?**

The product may present observed counts, transaction types, package shapes, positions acquired/disposed, draft-pick and FAAB activity, seasons observed, and counterparty counts. Presentation may sort, group, and summarize those facts without creating a second analytical authority.

It must not infer a durable preference, strategy label, willingness-to-pay coefficient, or acceptance probability unless a separately governed evidence contract explicitly promotes that inference. Small samples remain visibly small samples.

Behavioral Intelligence never alters universal Market Value, competitive Simulation, Trade Decision disposition, or Search authority by itself. It may provide contextual evidence to downstream governed consumers through explicit contracts.

The surface is league-agnostic and team-agnostic. League-specific history is input data, not embedded product logic. No league size, lineup format, scoring rule, team identity, draft convention, or owner behavior is hardcoded into the presentation.
