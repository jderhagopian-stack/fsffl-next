# Contextual Owner Intelligence — integrated delivery milestone

Management agreed to one end-to-end outcome rather than treating individual
historical adapters as product milestones. This continues the standing roadmap;
it does not start a history rewrite, new valuation method or paid service.

## Owner-visible outcome

Open an owner's profile, inspect a real past trade, understand the supported
circumstances, and see exactly what the completed decision establishes. The
product leads with the exchange, recorded team situation and useful roster facts.
Research lineage stays internal. A single precedent does not become a durable
preference or an acceptance percentage.

## One delivery track and acceptance gate

Keep the implementation track open until these connected checks are satisfied:

1. Source → historical case: exact event/season/owner binding, both action clocks,
   roster/pick/rule context, recorded production/results, and scoped unknowns.
   Reuse the existing reconstruction/normalization owners and source receipts.
2. Case → interpretation: every statement follows from an identified fact;
   missing fields narrow the statement. No invented contender label, replacement
   player, historical forecast, complete pick inventory or owner intention.
3. Interpretation → validation: real pre-draft, ordinary in-season, mixed-pick,
   multi-team and incomplete cases pass source-grounding checks. Predictive
   tendencies require a grouped chronological comparison against pooled/context
   baselines; an unavailable or unsuccessful model remains unavailable.
4. Case → product: carry compact, source-bound cases through the existing profile
   persistence/API into Owner Intelligence. Verify owner isolation, refresh,
   restore, mobile readability and working drill-downs. GET readers never build
   history. No case data may be silently fabricated for a live league.
5. Release: exact stable-head checks, measured bounded resource effects, real-data
   browser journey and explicit hosted/deployed acceptance. Physical iPhone
   acceptance remains distinct. A PR or preview alone does not close the milestone.

Use one consolidated draft implementation branch. Internal helpers may change;
do not merge them independently and call each a user-visible milestone.

## Reuse and boundaries

PRs #593 and #594 supply draft-aware membership and the factual circumstance join.
The accepted Behavioral expectation/residual methods and the existing Decision
bridge remain the foundations. The native 21-feature model remains unchanged;
partial facts cannot become full inputs through zero-filling. Any feature
extension is explicitly versioned research before stronger live weights.

Recorded `creator` identifies the initiating account. `consenter_ids` are roster
IDs, not account IDs. Require an explicit admitted roster/account binding before
attributing that roster's asset direction to the account; retain this narrow basis
without claiming its complete tenure or every counterparty's identity.

Completed trades provide positive observations only. They do not supply rejected
offers, an actual available-offer set, or calibrated acceptance probabilities.
Current circumstances and a user's explicit priorities stay separate from history.

Use existing Render/Supabase/GitHub resources. No new recurring expense, service,
connection adapter, cache or infrastructure change is authorized. Do not expose
research fixture data as production evidence or store raw sources in public PRs.

The native 21-input model remains incomplete for these archive contexts. The
factual review does not bypass that gate. A failed/unavailable research inference
must stay unavailable; factual examples can still be useful. Never describe this
checkpoint as completion of the entire Owner Intelligence milestone.

Historical cases carry an activity-prefix fingerprint as well as their event and
source hashes. Refresh may preserve them after genuinely later activity, but a
newly discovered earlier event invalidates the affected source window. Current
position-label enrichment does not rewrite dated historical production evidence.
The initiating account and admitted season roster binding are the narrow
attribution basis; no complete tenure or counterparty witness is claimed.

`product/historical_owner_delivery.py` composes already admitted inputs offline.
It does not fetch or publish automatically. `scripts/review_contextual_owner_delivery.py`
uses supplied API-shaped profiles with the actual browser component for local
review; it is not a new hosted service or a source of live league data. The
existing profile store and read API remain the delivery owners. No raw fixture,
profile payload, SQLite review database or screenshot is committed to this PR.
