## Executive decision

- **GO (software license only):** MIT-coded `nflreadpy`, `nflreadr`, `nflfastR`, `ffsimulator`, `nflseedR` under their respective applicable notices; code eligibility does **not** mean a linked data pipeline is cleared. No current application integration approved.
- **CONDITIONAL (software only):** `ffopportunity` GPLv3 code may be run exclusively on a private hosted server without conveying it; no obligation under ordinary GPLv3 to disclose source arises merely because users interact over the network. Must verify architecture and distribution boundary (including frontend / mobile / client code, containers/third-party handoffs) before treating as eligible; never distribute a combined GPL-covered FSFFL client requiring FSFFL source release. `ffopportunity` pretrained models and expected-point files are separate CC BY-SA 4.0 assets, NOT cleared by GPL code rights.
- **HOLD (datasets):** nflverse released player stats, PBP, player IDs/rosters, injuries, depth charts, schedules, FTN participation/charting, and FFverse modeled expected points; confirm upstream owners and allowed uses, including paid SaaS, internal storage, point-in-time model training, and displayed numeric outputs. Some have stronger explicit licenses than others. No general production clearance.
- **NO-GO (acquisition/use under identified provider terms):** Direct noncommercial-only NFL.com service data collection for our paid product without authorization; direct Sports Reference content used for FSFFL ML model training, given express prohibited AI/ML use under current posted terms; any distribution that requires disclosure of FSFFL application code or license payments; NC-licensed artifacts. How restrictions apply to independently acquired factual information or third-party mirrors may differ, but **those mirrors are HOLD, not assumed NO-GO or cleared**.
- **NEXT ACTION:** retain the historical pilot #458 at a legal/rights hold for its proposed source data until the affected sources or independently obtained statistics satisfy the exact-use rights gate. Permissively licensed code and public method documentation may be reviewed in research now. Do not queue parallel Codex implementation; Career #405 remains separate.

**Important source licensing nuance:** Sports Reference expressly says factual information itself cannot be copyrighted, while its **site terms** limit certain access and uses of the site's content. The NFL's site terms likewise apply to its services; they do **not automatically prove that all independently obtained numerical sports facts are prohibited**. This review does not determine whether a particular copy is governed by a specific contract. That is exactly why mirrored/PBP sources are **HOLD** rather than declaring all football statistics unavailable.

## Exact boundary conditions for proceeding to quantitative pilot

**Safe to proceed immediately with:** documentation/code license review of MIT code and GPL statistical methods; writing synthetic benchmarks or independently implemented modeling interfaces using fully original/synthetic data; retrospective desk analysis of public source documentation. This is not the planned raw NFLverse player coverage pilot.

**Do not do until rights evidence is adequate:** acquiring uncertain-rights `stats_player`/PBP data for product-model training; importing CC BY-SA pretrained weights into proprietary model pipeline without derivative analysis; using PFR-derived snaps/draft datasets for training merely because they are mirrored; downloading restricted NFL.com data commercially; copying NFL imagery.

**For a genuine first NFLverse data pilot**, the earliest candidate is basic weekly/seasonal numeric player statistics under the repository's CC BY 4.0 label. Before any **production** or **product model** adoption, obtain a legal conclusion grounded in source provenance and specific uses without special commercial licenses. A narrow personal research use may have different rights, but #458 explicitly requested rights review *before proceeding*, so do not start source-data acquisition now.

**Practical STOP rule:** If existing terms and reliable provenance cannot support our exact paid and proprietary use without fees or source disclosure, mark **NO-GO for this integration**, and switch to legally independent factual sources or our own derived models. Neither a completed research backtest nor a ChatGPT recommendation alone is commercial-rights clearance.
