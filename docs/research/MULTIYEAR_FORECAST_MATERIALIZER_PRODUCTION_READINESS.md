# Bounded Multi-Year Forecast Materializer — Production-Readiness Review

**Status:** research/shadow only; non-authoritative; no production Forecast change; no production PR; PR #131 remains research provenance, draft, open, and unmerged.

## Executive conclusion

**Recommendation: DO NOT PROMOTE the tested materializer.**

This review tested exactly one predeclared, Forecast-owned current-player career-transition selection/materialization policy. The candidate deliberately reused the existing empirical career-transition dimensions, applied one deterministic fallback, updated player state only from forecasted information, and imposed generic empirical safety bounds before final holdout results were read.

The candidate successfully solved the most obvious trajectory-explosion problem: it produced **100% complete** historical paths on the evaluable universe, had **zero structural production-bound violations**, preserved rank ordering, and delivered nominal-80% coverage of approximately **81.5% in Year 2** and **82.3% in Year 3**.

But it fails the production-readiness gates that matter most. Year-2 MAE improves only **0.5%** versus a simple Year-1 carry-forward control, cumulative path MAE is **1.2% worse**, only **4 of 18** chronological folds improve cumulatively, all-QB cumulative MAE is **5.8% worse**, and elite-QB cumulative MAE is **16.7% worse**. The empirical survival probabilities also miss the predeclared Brier gate.

The elite-QB result is a hard failure under the management contract. The generic bounded selector removes unsupported explosions but does not make the deeper QB path accurate enough for authoritative downstream use.

Per the stopping rule, no second hierarchy, QB-specific rule, alternate shrinkage family, new transition model, or post-hoc bound was tried. No current-player live shadow was run because historical hard gates did not pass. No production PR was created.

## 2. Exact candidate selection/fallback policy

The candidate was frozen before final holdout inspection.

### Narrow evidence

Use the existing calibration cell:

`position × age × experience × production quartile × rookie status`

only when that cell contains at least:

- **30 historical transition rows**, and
- **15 survivors**.

### Deterministic fallback

If the exact cell is unsupported, use the pre-existing **position parent** evidence only when it contains at least:

- **100 historical transition rows**, and
- **50 survivors**.

If the supported position parent does not exist, the path fails closed. There is no player override, QB-specific fallback, market fallback, global hand-tuned aging curve, or downstream Value fallback.

The same rule applies to every player and historical fold.

## 3. State update between horizons

After each materialized transition:

- age increments by one year when known;
- experience increments by one;
- rookie status applies only when pre-step experience is zero and then expires;
- position remains fixed;
- production quartile is recomputed from the **forecasted next-horizon means within position**;
- survival remains represented probabilistically inside the Forecast distribution and cumulative survival evidence.

No realized Year-2 or Year-3 production is used to choose the next transition. The production-state reclassification therefore uses only information generated by the prior Forecast step.

## 4. Predeclared safety/bounding rules

The materializer uses generic empirical bounds rather than famous-player or positional exceptions.

### Transition multiplier support

For each position and historical fold, conditional production multipliers are restricted to the **5th–95th percentile of strictly prior, position-specific year-level transition slopes**.

### Production-jump support

Conditional active production may not exceed:

`max(current Forecast mean, prior position 99th-percentile realized production)`.

This permits an already-elite player to retain an elite level while preventing a lower forecast from jumping beyond historical position support.

### Decline/survival

- production retains the natural zero floor;
- survival remains empirical and bounded to `[0,1]` by the existing Forecast evidence type;
- unsupported parent evidence fails closed.

No special QB cap, superstar multiplier, aging penalty, momentum term, or market information is used.

## 5. Predeclared acceptance gates

Before final holdout inspection the study required:

- Year-2 MAE improvement >= **2%** versus Year-1 carry-forward;
- Year-3 MAE improvement >= **2%**;
- cumulative MAE improvement >= **3%**;
- cumulative improvement in >= **60%** of chronological folds;
- no fold worse than **10%**;
- Year-2 and Year-3 Spearman degradation no worse than **0.02**;
- all-QB cumulative MAE no more than **2% worse** than carry-forward;
- elite-QB cumulative MAE no more than **2% worse**;
- RB/WR/TE cumulative MAE no more than **5% worse**;
- overall nominal-80% coverage in **70–90%** for Years 2 and 3;
- sufficiently large positional groups in **65–95%** coverage;
- Year-2/Year-3 survival Brier <= **0.25**;
- completeness >= **95%** overall and >= **90%** by position;
- zero structural extremeness-bound violations;
- explicit provenance and no future leakage.

QB safety was a hard gate.

## 11. Trajectory extremeness

The structural bounds did what they were designed to do:

- **0** paths exceeded the predeclared production-support ceiling;
- completeness was **100%** overall and for QB/RB/WR/TE;
- no player-specific override was required.

**Trajectory-extremeness gate: PASS.**

This is a useful negative result. The earlier spectacular QB explosions can be structurally prevented without a QB-specific cap, but preventing explosions alone does not establish predictive authority.

## 12. Uncertainty and survival

### Uncertainty

The candidate uses production `apply_career_transition(...)`, including recursive incoming-variance carry-forward, empirical transition dispersion, estimation uncertainty, and the survival mixture.

Nominal-80% historical interval coverage is:

- Year 2: **81.5%**;
- Year 3: **82.3%**.

Position-level coverage also remains within the predeclared safety envelope for sufficiently large groups.

**Uncertainty calibration: PASS.**

### Survival

The aggregate Brier scores are:

- Year 2: **0.268**;
- Year 3: **0.260**.

Both exceed the predeclared <=0.25 gate.

QB survival alone is better calibrated (~0.246 / ~0.247), while the aggregate miss is driven more by non-QB groups. Survival remains correctly Forecast-owned, but this exact evidence/materialization candidate does not clear the frozen survival-performance bar.

**Survival behavior: FAIL.**

But this candidate does not earn the right to instantiate that object as authoritative production truth.

Therefore:

- no production materializer is added;
- no Y1/Y2/Y3 production contract is promoted;
- no cache/version wiring is introduced;
- Value continues to fail closed for missing authoritative multi-year Forecast evidence;
- Model A production implementation remains blocked.

## Management recommendation

**DO NOT PROMOTE this materializer.**

The result is useful and bounded rather than ambiguous:

1. the existing production career-transition recursion can be used safely without numerical trajectory explosions when empirical support bounds are applied;
2. Year-3 signal is materially useful, and RB/TE performance is encouraging;
3. nevertheless, the single frozen selector/materializer is not accurate or chronologically stable enough as a complete authoritative Y1/Y2/Y3 path;
4. most importantly, it materially worsens QB and elite-QB deep-horizon accuracy;
5. survival calibration also misses the frozen gate.

Per the stopping rule, this review does **not** try a second hierarchy, alternate transition family, stronger shrinkage, different bounds, or QB-specific logic.
