# FSFFL NEXT — NFLverse basic historical statistics: upstream contractual-reach decision

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-14. **Program:** [Research #458](https://github.com/jderhagopian-stack/fsffl-next/issues/458).  
**Question decided:** Does NFL.com / another upstream source's website terms **automatically** bind a downstream FSFFL NEXT user of **already-published NFLverse GitHub historical numerical stats**—and therefore compel an NFL commercial data license, prohibit a paid proprietary service, or require source-code disclosure?

## Decision (specific, not general clearance)

**NO AUTOMATIC PASS-THROUGH OF NFL.COM WEBSITE TERMS WAS ESTABLISHED.** Based on published terms, the NFL.com agreement defines and regulates use of NFL-operated **Services**. It expressly prohibits nonconsensual direct commercial/systematic retrieval *from those Services*. It does not, on its face, establish a downstream contract with every independent person accessing NFLverse-released facts from GitHub. Actual assent, acquisition path, and any other applicable agreement are material to a contract claim; this conclusion is not a ruling that redistribution or use can never be challenged.

**CONDITIONAL GO FOR A BOUNDED, OFFLINE RESEARCH VALIDATION OF BASIC HISTORICAL NUMERICAL PLAYER STATISTICS** downloaded from the NFLverse GitHub public `stats_player` release, using the source's publicly asserted CC BY 4.0 grant, a clear contributor attribution record, **no raw redistribution or paid product display**, **no NFL.com or PFR scraping**, and no production authority changes. Restrict initially to **2024 and 2025 completed-season regular-season and weekly counting statistics**. Expand 1999–2025 after valid source checksum, player joins, and demonstrated coverage; keep 2026 partial and PIT/source-revision effects separate. This decision means the research pilot is *eligible to run*, not that it has run or that a lawyer issued source title assurance.

**PRODUCTION / PAID-CUSTOMER DISPLAY OR TRAINED-MODEL PROMOTION: STILL CONDITIONAL.** The CC BY 4.0 dataset license grants commercial uses to the extent the licensor holds applicable rights; it has no license fees, source-sharing obligations for unrelated proprietary FSFFL application code, or free-product requirement. Neither the public NFLverse repo label nor the uncopyrightability of facts proves that the full licensed compilation / every original NFL-derived item was cleared by the originating service. Review the narrow production integration with a qualified IP/data-license attorney if commercial risk must be certified; check specific contracts to which FSFFL or our data supplier are actually bound. This is **not** a prerequisite to limited isolated research. Do not make paid-launch claims or import production data until that decision is recorded.

### Exact authority and limitations

1. **NFL.com Terms §1.3**: https://www.nfl.com/legal/terms/ (updated **2024-05-16**). The terms begin by defining the covered *Services* as websites/apps/other online services operated by NFL Enterprises. §1.3 forbids commercial use and systematic data retrieval from those Services without written consent. **Result:** FSFFL must not commercially scrape NFL.com or treat a personal NFL.com account as a data license. It does **not follow** that our independent download from NFLverse GitHub automatically constitutes use of NFL.com Services or acceptance of NFL.com's terms. This review has not found any separate FSFFL assent to NFL data terms.
2. **NFLverse data license**: https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/blob/main/LICENSE.md ; **CC BY 4.0** https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ explicitly authorizes commercial copying/adaptation, with attribution, license link and modification notices. **Limits:** rights granted only where licensor has them; no upstream noninfringement or ownership warranty. A license statement is credible first-party evidence, **not blanket proof of ownership in every item**.
3. **NFLverse loader terms:** https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/ disclaims rights over underlying NFL data and refers to original owners' terms. `nflfastR` package DESCRIPTION specifically identifies NFL.com play-by-play as the raw original source. This creates **provenance diligence**, but it is not itself a contract by which FSFFL agreed to NFL.com's website terms.
4. **`Feist`, 499 U.S. 340**: https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/499/340 . Bare historical facts are generally not protected by copyright even when part of a database; original selection or arrangement may be. NFL season/week player game totals are strong examples of *facts*, not a claim that the CSV's complete structure is incapable of protection.
5. **`NBA v. Motorola`, 105 F.3d 841 (2d Cir. 1997)**: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/105/841/598844/ . Game results/real-time scores (and factual sport data) are distinct from copyrighted broadcasts; the court rejected that particular sports-data claim. It involved separately gathered data; it does **not** directly decide NFLverse redistribution or its original collector's NFL.com contract.
6. **`ProCD v. Zeidenberg`, 86 F.3d 1447 (7th Cir. 1996)**: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F3/86/1447/538242/ . The facts may be unprotected, but a contract accepted by the acquirer can independently restrict its use. **No-contract-presumption** cuts both ways: do not assume *NFLverse* did not accept its upstream terms; do not assume *FSFFL* is automatically an NFL.com contracting party solely because the GitHub numbers originated there.
7. **NFLverse public commercial-rights inquiry**: https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/issues/101 , filed 2026-09-12 and closed 2026-10-02 without substantive response. This is **neither a denial nor an affirmative warranty**.
8. **Collection routes**: official `stats_player` release at https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/stats_player is distributed **through GitHub**. The code https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-pbp/blob/master/R/update_stats.R uses `nflfastR::calculate_stats`, which loads released PBP **and** playstats, themselves originally rooted in NFL.com data. GitHub publication does not retroactively absolve the original collector of any agreement, and a downstream recipient is not *automatically* an original-service user.

## Use-by-use determination

| FSFFL action | Finding |
|---|---|
| Charge users for FSFFL's independent service | **ALLOWED by CC BY 4.0 itself**; no license obligation to make FSFFL free |
| Preserve proprietary application source code / forecast intellectual property | **ALLOWED by CC BY 4.0 itself** (not a copyleft source-code license) |
| Download publicly released `stats_player` CSV from GitHub for a tiny isolated research audit | **CONDITIONAL RESEARCH GO** under stated license and scope, with provenance and attribution preserved; not a ruling on original NFL collection contract |
| Store the licensed basic factual numerical subset and compute internally for research | **CONDITIONAL RESEARCH GO** with no public redistribution, separate controlled workspace and source hash |
| Train customer-facing model on entire multi-decade NFLverse archive | **PRODUCTION HOLD** pending actual field/data rights and compliance analysis; research benchmark results can inform later decision |
| Display historical player statistics alongside original proprietary predictions in paid SaaS | CC BY grant **permits this in principle**; **PRODUCTION HOLD** for this specific dataset's upstream-rights/integration check and notices |
| Scrape NFL.com directly; ingest copyrighted highlight footage, logos, images or licensed FTN/PFR charts | **NO-GO** under this pilot |
| Claim an NFL endorsement or sell bulk republished NFLverse CSV files | **OUT OF SCOPE / NO-GO** |

**Bottom line:** External public GitHub origin and a commercial-permissive CC BY grant support proceeding with an isolated factual-coverage research pilot. It would be inaccurate to assert that the underlying original data chain is therefore fully indemnified or already cleared for paid production use. Distinguish that residual legal risk from eligibility to run research.
