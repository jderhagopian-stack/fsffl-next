# Current roster screening coordinates

For native ROS evidence, the pre-Simulation bilateral screen consumes the existing
bye-aware weekly scoring owner over each team's remaining regular-season games.
It applies the same legal trade and mandatory cuts first. It does not turn a sum
of season/ROS player totals into a weekly team contribution.

The screen declares `current_points_basis`, `current_points_unit`, the union of
weeks and each team's scheduled weeks. `starter_points_delta` is the difference
in scheduled weekly fantasy-point means under this explicit basis. The separate
`position_strength_comparisons` remains a total-horizon lineup diagnostic; it is
not silently relabeled as weekly evidence. Legacy full-season/preseason screens
declare `optimized_horizon_lineup` and retain their existing behavior.

`product/current_roster_screen.py` requires exact source-State forward evidence.
It can reuse the published baseline weekly panel only when canonical State,
Forecast fingerprint and all required weekly coordinates match. Otherwise it
derives that baseline through the same weekly scorer and exact Forecast. Changed
scoring is bounded to the two affected teams; neither branch runs Simulation,
acquires providers or writes an artifact. Missing source/schedule/scoring evidence
withholds that current screen rather than falling back to preseason or a floor.

Market economics, existing materiality bands, legal cuts, Forecast means and
uncertainty, availability, weekly volatility and final Decision authority remain
upstream. Positive weekly means are not positive expected wins or acceptance.
Both effects and sacrifices remain visible, and final recommendations still need
the established Simulation and long-term authority gates.

Discovery retains its existing 640-visit and eight-screen limits. This correction
does not promote a new selector or candidate-generation strategy. Retention changes
require same-input usefulness and cost evidence before default activation.
