## Guiding principles inherited from the charter

1. **Validated superiority, not legacy parity.** Old FSFFL is a research corpus and source of historical algorithms, not new semantic authority.
2. **One-way authority:** Data → exact PIT State → Forecast → Value → Team Utility/Simulation/Decision → Search → Analytics/API → Presentation. State does not create Value; historical reports never invent Decision grades.
3. **Truth of the historical decision:** what was knowable then is separate from what happened later; no future-leaking transactions, present-day projections or hindsight draft slots. Track `effective_at`, original-source `available_at`, actual acquisition, provenance and model version distinctly. Unknown source availability is not proof it existed at the cutoff.
4. **Multi-league and season-specific:** team counts, eligible positions (including K/DST, flex/SF where configured), roster sizes, scoring, lineup, bench/taxi/IR, draft rounds, playoffs, keeper/dynasty conventions, FAAB, season length, auction/startup context, trades and eligibility must come from each league's own timestamped rules and data. Do not hardcode FSFFL's 12 teams, 3 WR, Superflex, 2022 startup treatment, team names or Sleeper ID. Rules can change mid-season; source/State revision determines what was valid at a cutoff.
5. **Changing league identity:** a provider may assign a fresh league ID each season. A `league_family_id` links explicitly verified season IDs; no implicit assumption of stable rosters, franchise owners, fantasy team names, or manager identity across seasons. Historical identity crosswalks need their own verifiable evidence.
6. **Scale and cost:** seek widest mathematically safe reuse—global NFL facts, provider observations, scoring/rules signatures, league State, team/user decisions respectively—while keeping private tenant material isolated. Immutable once accepted; idempotent writes, bounded connections, metadata-first reads, indexed lookups, and no full multi-league reacquisition every sign-in. The intended architecture must be plausible at 100k leagues and evolve to 1m without a rewrite; don't run huge tests in a constrained beta.
7. **Security and source rights:** no private league payloads or third-party restricted data in GitHub; every source needs licensed use/availability documentation. Research-only data cannot be silently promoted to private-beta or commercial production.
8. **Useful outputs, scoped testing:** a backend capability must be deterministically testable without a browser and ultimately make a real owner job better. The presentation remains a thin reader with simple visual discovery and evidence drill-down.

## Configuration-compatibility reality (must be made explicit)

The existing canonical Python `Position` enum currently represents QB/RB/WR/TE/K/DST, and `RosterSlot` represents those plus FLEX/SUPERFLEX/BENCH/TAXI/IR. This **does not yet represent every league configuration**: IDP (DL/LB/DB/CB/S/EDGE), custom positional designations, multi-position eligibility, special keeper/contract/auction/salary rules, unusual playoff formats, and provider-specific scoring/stat categories need an explicit provider-to-canonical capability matrix before use. `LeagueRules.scoring` is adaptable in shape, but a stored scoring rule is **not** evidence that every model has the underlying stat forecast or simulation semantics. An unmapped material rule must be reported as UNSUPPORTED/PARTIAL; do not discard it, coerce it into offensive positions, or silently fabricate scores. The proper extension is to the canonical State/Forecast/Value/Simulation contracts with versioned tests, **not** a league-specific exception or a new UI-side calculator. All formats must preserve source and historical effective date. Successful tests of 2/8/10/12/14/16 conventional leagues prove variable-size mechanics only, not universal capability for IDP or auction/salary leagues.

## First implementation slice: historical cutoff evidence inventory (in draft)

`src/fsffl/state/historical_coverage.py` adds a **read-only, provider-neutral** historical season identity and evidence receipt/cutoff inventory. It deliberately makes no network calls, SQL queries, artifacts, model scores or publication writes. It validates that evidence belongs to the requested season/league family, selects no post-cutoff State, retains actual per-season scoring and roster settings and their fingerprint, and marks missing, unverified, or future-only evidence explicitly. It is NOT a historical State materializer; does not certify historical State source completeness; and even full receipt coverage does not authorize a historical decision grade. Inputs represent externally validated historical facts and availability, not self-certified provider timestamps.

Independent synthetic fixture matrix covers 2/8/10/12/14/16-team structures, scoring changes, multiple roster/position/round arrangements, family-linked changing season IDs, cutoff fences, missing older seasons, no admissible historical evidence, source-availability ambiguity, timezone equivalence and cross-tenant/mixed-season refusal. Existing State/Trade tests must continue to pass.

**No production permission:** branch-only module, tests, workstream doc and CI. Do not merge this as a feature without code review/accepted promotion gate. No production data writes, migrations, worker flag, Render/Supabase changes, API or UI change.

## Gates before advancing

- Tier A first-slice contract tests, exact-head focused CI and independent schema review; no need to exercise beta for a dormant state-only helper.
- Historical acquisition gate: documented provider rules, season-family and team crosswalk, source availability/rights and a complete missing-data ledger using at least two structurally different synthetic leagues plus one real private league sample in a secured environment.
- Real historical materializer gate: original-transaction and pick ownership parity for sampled years/dates, immutable snapshot identity, replay/restart, memory/DB budget, no future data and no arbitrary team/roster/position constants.
- Decision/retrospective gate: one full historical trade with explicit uncertainty, lineage and independent at-time vs realized results; block grades where authority absent.
- UI/hosting gate comes *after* model correctness; one small read-only API/query is sufficient initially. No new service/cost until bounded resource evidence.
