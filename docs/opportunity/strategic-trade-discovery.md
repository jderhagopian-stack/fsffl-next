# Strategic trade discovery and optimization

## Product direction

Explore discovers strong, realistically investigable exchanges for a franchise by
working from team circumstances, owner objectives and economic opportunities to
candidate transactions. Trade Center evaluates selected transactions in depth.
Build on the existing Forecast, Value, Team Utility, bilateral Decision,
Opportunity Search, Owner Intelligence, Simulation and persistence authorities.

## Default inference and user direction

The default experience infers likely priorities for each owner from available
team circumstances and supported owner evidence. Inference includes provenance,
freshness, uncertainty and missing evidence. A calculated competitive state is a
fact about model outputs; a suggested strategic priority is an interpretation.
Neither a weak record nor a strong position group establishes an owner's wishes
or expendable supply by itself. Unknown preferences remain explicitly uncertain.

The managed user can direct a search using chosen priorities and constraints,
including multiple objectives, protected assets, targets, pick restrictions and
acceptable current/future sacrifices. Explicit user directions take precedence
over inferred priorities for that search; they do not rewrite calculated team
state, Forecast, universal Value or Decision consequences. Returning to automatic
mode restores evidence-based inference. Preserve the distinction between durable
owner preferences, a temporary search instruction, and a hypothetical assumption
about another owner. Do not present a user's assumption about a counterparty as
that counterparty's stated preference.

Owner evidence improves relevance without fabricating acceptance probabilities.
Existing contextual/residual preference models require their accepted evidence
and calibration gates before activation. Completed trades alone do not establish
how often an owner would accept all offers they might receive.

## Discovery and economics

Generate independent hypotheses for marginal lineup/roster improvement,
current-versus-future exchanges, attractive or expensive economic contribution
relative to market standing, consolidation/distribution, and explicit user intent.
A target can be relevant without filling a weak position. Market evidence informs
plausible acquisition costs and negotiating context; equal market totals are not
the objective.

Evaluate both teams' actual before/after consequences, opportunity costs,
required cuts, depth, flexibility, uncertainty and stated/inferred objectives.
Preserve separate current competitive, 3-Year, Career and market channels. Use
compatible raw authorities; independent display indexes cannot be subtracted to
produce economic discounts. Overlapping 3-Year/Career quantities cannot be added
or subtracted to invent a new time horizon. Missing evidence is not zero or a
substitute Market value. Do not add Search-owned scarcity or consolidation
premiums to effects already present upstream.

Mutual benefit is objective- and team-specific. Shared additive asset-value
changes remain received-minus-sent; an exchange need not increase both owners'
universal asset totals to serve different circumstances or time preferences.
Mixed outcomes require an explicit governed preference interpretation or remain
conditional for review. Search cannot grant itself new Decision authority.

Reuse general legal bilateral package contracts, including received picks,
player/pick mixes, two-for-two and distribution structures where supported.
Preserve several non-dominated alternatives without a hidden master score.
“Strongest found” is qualified by objectives, evidence and search coverage;
unknown owner willingness and bounded search do not support a global-optimum or
acceptance claim.

## Incremental development order

1. Verify evidence delivery end to end. Reuse accepted methods; identify actual
   consumer, horizon, coverage and identity gaps before reopening model research.
2. Connect existing raw Intrinsic to compatible horizon-specific package/Decision
   evidence. Make inferred versus explicit objectives auditable. Preserve existing
   useful partial paths and current published snapshots.
3. Extend bounded single-trade discovery across independent strategic/economic
   hypotheses, complementary franchises and broader package structures.
4. Review a mobile Explore mockup, then integrate exact exchanges, both-team
   rationale, sacrifices, evidence status, targeted Trade Center simulation and
   counteroffers. Validate real-league usefulness and failure states.
5. Expand supported owner-specific negotiation intelligence and local package
   optimization; later consider contingent multi-transaction strategy. Unsecured
   follow-up transactions cannot be credited as completed benefits.

A complete franchise utility estimator, acceptance model, new worker or provider
plan is not a prerequisite for a useful first release. New preference rules,
valuation conversions or calibrations require their own evidence/review; this
roadmap does not authorize arbitrary weights, cutoffs or model replacement.

## Runtime and acceptance

Reuse published baselines, exact scenario reuse/coalescing, existing bounded
resource ownership and persistence. Scope shared work to its widest safe semantic
dependencies while preserving tenant-private State. Broad screening is cheap;
expensive scenario confirmation is selective. No unbounded league-wide package
cross-product, hidden heavy work on page reads, or duplicated lifecycle owner.

Validate contracts with bounded fixtures, then compare discovery using the same
retained real-league inputs. Include good and bad cases for both-team objectives,
market/Intrinsic disagreement, thin strong rooms, received picks, 2-for-2,
consolidation/distribution, cuts, uncertainty, unknown preferences and explicit
user overrides. Report generation/screening/truncation coverage, useful-candidate
survival, duplicate concentration, latency, peak/retained memory and expensive
work counts. Historical outcomes are not invented point-in-time forecasts.

Preserve existing feature coverage, snapshots, accepted source/model rules,
league-agnostic rules and authority boundaries. Follow focused regression,
reviewed exact-head CI/full-suite and targeted hosted/browser acceptance for each
release. Merge/startup alone does not certify strategic usefulness or physical
journey completion.

A coherent saved State alone does not establish current injury-adjusted inputs.

## Progressive discovery delivery

Automatic For You and directed Trade Finder use one strategic-discovery engine.
Trade Finder is the configure-first entry, including a required outgoing player or
pick; Explore supports the same targets, counterparties, position and protection
constraints. Trade Center remains the deeper bilateral/Simulation workspace.
Legacy browser search code remains a compatibility fallback, not the live second
engine. Removing that fallback is a later cleanup, not a new economic model.

Evaluate at most eight distinct packages per request and at most 32 per search.
Continue automatically toward four preliminary fits, or stop when the bounded
pool or screening limit is reached. Four is a shortlist-size preference, not an
acceptance/materiality threshold or guarantee of benefit. Additional screening
within the remaining budget is explicit; the user can pause after a batch.
Completed packages remain visible and are not re-evaluated by continuation.

Continuation is signed and tied to tenant, State, published generation, evidence
identities and instructions. A small, byte/entry/TTL-bounded response cache reuses
exact repeats without retaining State/model graphs or creating persistence.
Cheap bounded generation is replayed between batches; detailed screens are skipped
by canonical IDs. One repair sequence is shared across the whole search. Missing
or conflicting evidence, failed parents, protections and exact handoffs remain
intact. Restart/expiration or evidence changes require a new search.

The next pick tranche is governed by [pick-economics-coverage.md](pick-economics-coverage.md).
Current Market and origin-slot evidence is reusable; compatible native-horizon
pick contribution must pass its own upstream authority/evidence gate.
