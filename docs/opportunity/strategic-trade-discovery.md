# Strategic trade discovery and optimization

## Product direction

Explore discovers strong, realistically investigable exchanges for a franchise by
working from team circumstances, owner objectives and economic opportunities to
candidate transactions. Trade Center evaluates selected transactions in depth.
Build on the existing Forecast, Value, Team Utility, bilateral Decision,
Opportunity Search, Owner Intelligence, Simulation and persistence authorities.

## Default inference and user direction

The default experience infers likely priorities for each owner from available
team circumstances and supported owner evidence. Inference includes provenance,
freshness, uncertainty and missing evidence. A calculated competitive state is a
fact about model outputs; a suggested strategic priority is an interpretation.
Neither a weak record nor a strong position group establishes an owner's wishes
or expendable supply by itself. Unknown preferences remain explicitly uncertain.

The managed user can direct a search using chosen priorities and constraints,
including multiple objectives, protected assets, targets, pick restrictions and
acceptable current/future sacrifices. Explicit user directions take precedence
over inferred priorities for that search; they do not rewrite calculated team
state, Forecast, universal Value or Decision consequences. Returning to automatic
mode restores evidence-based inference. Preserve the distinction between durable
owner preferences, a temporary search instruction, and a hypothetical assumption
about another owner. Do not present a user's assumption about a counterparty as
that counterparty's stated preference.

Directed discovery supports combinations such as “players aged 26 or younger
and draft picks, WR when receiving players, protect these assets, and compare
Current plus Career.” Asset kinds are alternatives; player age and position are
intersecting filters, combined with partner, offered asset and protections.
Age 26 means younger than the 27th birthday in canonical point-in-time State;
unknown ages do not satisfy an explicit age filter. Picks have no player age or
position. Incoming assets, including feedback repairs, must satisfy the requested
target filters. A conflicting specific target is rejected rather than silently
relaxing the request. Existing position-only callers keep player-only behavior.

Users may choose several horizon priorities. Those objectives remain separate in
Decision; no new blended score, valuation premium or acceptance probability is
introduced. In automatic mode, asset filters can still constrain discovery while
objective inference remains active. Find More retains the same request settings,
and its existing signed continuation fingerprint rejects changed settings.
Pick targeting does not admit unvalidated native pick values: absent pick
Intrinsic remains unavailable until the separate research gate passes.

Owner evidence improves relevance without fabricating acceptance probabilities.
Existing contextual/residual preference models require their accepted evidence
and calibration gates before activation. Completed trades alone do not establish
how often an owner would accept all offers they might receive.

## Discovery and economics

Generate independent hypotheses for marginal lineup/roster improvement,
current-versus-future exchanges, attractive or expensive economic contribution
relative to market standing, consolidation/distribution, and explicit user intent.
A target can be relevant without filling a weak position. Market evidence informs
plausible acquisition costs and negotiating context; equal market totals are not
the objective.

Evaluate both teams' actual before/after consequences, opportunity costs,
required cuts, depth, flexibility, uncertainty and stated/inferred objectives.
Preserve separate current competitive, 3-Year, Career and market channels. Use
compatible raw authorities; independent display indexes cannot be subtracted to
produce economic discounts. Overlapping 3-Year/Career quantities cannot be added
or subtracted to invent a new time horizon. Missing evidence is not zero or a
substitute Market value. Do not add Search-owned scarcity or consolidation
premiums to effects already present upstream.

Mutual benefit is objective- and team-specific. Shared additive asset-value
changes remain received-minus-sent; an exchange need not increase both owners'
universal asset totals to serve different circumstances or time preferences.
Mixed outcomes require an explicit governed preference interpretation or remain
conditional for review. Search cannot grant itself new Decision authority.

Reuse general legal bilateral package contracts, including received picks,
player/pick mixes, two-for-two and distribution structures where supported.
Preserve several non-dominated alternatives without a hidden master score.
“Strongest found” is qualified by objectives, evidence and search coverage;
unknown owner willingness and bounded search do not support a global-optimum or
acceptance claim.

## Incremental development order

1. Verify evidence delivery end to end. Reuse accepted methods; identify actual
   consumer, horizon, coverage and identity gaps before reopening model research.
2. Connect existing raw Intrinsic to compatible horizon-specific package/Decision
   evidence. Make inferred versus explicit objectives auditable. Preserve existing
   useful partial paths and current published snapshots.
3. Extend bounded single-trade discovery across independent strategic/economic
   hypotheses, complementary franchises and broader package structures.
4. Review a mobile Explore mockup, then integrate exact exchanges, both-team
   rationale, sacrifices, evidence status, targeted Trade Center simulation and
   counteroffers. Validate real-league usefulness and failure states.
5. Expand supported owner-specific negotiation intelligence and local package
   optimization; later consider contingent multi-transaction strategy. Unsecured
   follow-up transactions cannot be credited as completed benefits.

A complete franchise utility estimator, acceptance model, new worker or provider
plan is not a prerequisite for a useful first release. New preference rules,
valuation conversions or calibrations require their own evidence/review; this
roadmap does not authorize arbitrary weights, cutoffs or model replacement.

## Runtime and acceptance

Reuse published baselines, exact scenario reuse/coalescing, existing bounded
resource ownership and persistence. Scope shared work to its widest safe semantic
dependencies while preserving tenant-private State. Broad screening is cheap;
expensive scenario confirmation is selective. No unbounded league-wide package
cross-product, hidden heavy work on page reads, or duplicated lifecycle owner.

Validate contracts with bounded fixtures, then compare discovery using the same
retained real-league inputs. Include good and bad cases for both-team objectives,
market/Intrinsic disagreement, thin strong rooms, received picks, 2-for-2,
consolidation/distribution, cuts, uncertainty, unknown preferences and explicit
user overrides. Report generation/screening/truncation coverage, useful-candidate
survival, duplicate concentration, latency, peak/retained memory and expensive
work counts. Historical outcomes are not invented point-in-time forecasts.

Preserve existing feature coverage, snapshots, accepted source/model rules,
league-agnostic rules and authority boundaries. Follow focused regression,
reviewed exact-head CI/full-suite and targeted hosted/browser acceptance for each
release. Merge/startup alone does not certify strategic usefulness or physical
journey completion.

## Progressive discovery delivery

Opportunities uses one strategic-discovery engine for automatic and directed
search, including a required outgoing player or pick, targets, counterparties,
position and protection constraints. Trade Center remains the deeper bilateral/Simulation workspace.
Legacy browser search code remains a compatibility fallback, not the live second
engine. Removing that fallback is a later cleanup, not a new economic model.

Evaluate at most eight distinct packages per request and at most 32 per action and 80 per search.
Continue automatically toward four preliminary fits, or stop when the bounded
pool or screening limit is reached. Four is a shortlist-size preference, not an
acceptance/materiality threshold or guarantee of benefit. Additional screening
within the remaining budget is explicit; the user can pause after a batch.
Completed packages remain visible and are not re-evaluated by continuation.

Continuation is signed and tied to tenant, State, published generation, evidence
identities and instructions. A small, byte/entry/TTL-bounded response cache reuses
exact repeats without retaining State/model graphs or creating persistence.
Cheap bounded generation is replayed between batches; detailed screens are skipped
by canonical IDs. One repair sequence is shared across the whole search. Missing
or conflicting evidence, failed parents, protections and exact handoffs remain
intact. Restart/expiration or evidence changes require a new search.

The next pick tranche is governed by [pick-economics-coverage.md](pick-economics-coverage.md).
Current Market and origin-slot evidence is reusable; compatible native-horizon
pick contribution must pass its own upstream authority/evidence gate.

## Unified Opportunities experience

Explore has three primary tabs: Opportunities, Player Board and Free Agents.
Opportunities combines automatic discovery and directed shopping; legacy For You
and Trade Finder links resolve to that same view. Trade Center remains full evaluation.

Find More keeps the current instructions and cursor, excludes earlier screened
canonical packages and retains their cards. Initial/action work is at most four
sequential eight-screen batches; explicit continuation can extend to80 distinct
screens per search. The server may stop earlier for a preliminary shortlist or
pool exhaustion. At80, all evaluated cards still fit the existing80-card retention
bound. Changing direction starts a separate search; previous exclusions are not
silently transferred to different evidence/settings. Ten-minute cursor expiry,
exact scope fences, bounded response reuse and fail-fast admission remain.

Explore different options opens the existing priorities/refinements without
submitting work or discarding results. Edits invalidate old cards until the user
submits the changed settings. Shop/Target actions remain explicit navigation.

Cards show Preliminary once, exact give/get, both-side effects and supported
roster/contribution explanations. Explanations summarize existing positional
lineup deltas and native horizon ledgers; they never infer expendable players,
owner acceptance or wins from lineup points. Unknown effects remain unavailable.
Method qualifications, source IDs, accounting and metric definitions are available
on demand. Actual errors, protected-player violations and required cuts remain visible.

## Larger bilateral package discovery

Strategic discovery now samples up to eight assets per side and ten assets total,
including player/pick combinations, larger consolidations and draft-pick bundles.
These are configurable engineering search bounds, not league transaction rules or
a change to player/pick Value. Trade Center can still evaluate a manually composed
legal package independently of the automatic discovery sample. Multi-team trade
discovery is separate work; this contract remains bilateral.

At most twelve ordered ingredients per side enter a lane. Required Shop/Target
assets are pinned before this ingredient limit. Per-cardinality combinations are
lazy, and diagonal pair streams retain only visited prefixes. The engine never
materializes the package universe or its full Cartesian product. The existing
640-pair/candidate pool limit, eight preliminary screens per request and bounded
Find More continuation remain unchanged. Larger packages do not cause additional
automatic Simulation runs or any Forecast/Intrinsic recalculation.

Shape scheduling alternates total size and send/receive orientation across lanes,
so the bounded sample includes both smaller and larger hypotheses. Dedicated
spend/acquire-draft-capital lanes provide sufficient pick ingredients for larger
pick bundles. These lanes consume the same upstream values, team context and user
constraints; they do not price assets or manufacture owner willingness.

Screening preserves existing economic-priority tiers. Within a tier it can prefer
an as-yet-unexamined package shape, alongside existing franchise/hypothesis/asset
diversity. A package gains no benefit or recommendation authority merely for being
larger. Every screened package still uses the existing roster legality, all required
cuts, raw horizon ledgers and both-team objectives. Unknown evidence remains unknown.
The existing narrow player-pair remove/add repair is not silently expanded into a
new mixed-asset optimizer. Generation and screened-shape diagnostics expose actual
sample coverage without claiming an exhaustive league optimum.

## Compact opportunity presentation and evaluation handoff

The default card shows all give/get assets in two compact columns and one signed
Current / 3-Year / Career comparison table for both franchises. Preliminary is the
screening label; required cuts, protected-player violations and known losses remain
visible. Team context, raw-contribution definitions and separate Market accounting
are available in details. Pick coverage text consumes the returned diagnostic,
rather than assuming native pick evidence is always unavailable. Search direction,
refinements and Find More remain available without creating a second search model.

Opening an opportunity in Trade Center transfers every asset reference, combined
chosen priorities and protected players. It copies the temporary instructions and
checks State, publication and current ownership before using the package. A changed
publication, stale State or any missing asset rejects the transfer. Trade Center's
existing quick draft, analysis, Simulation and frontier requests consume the same
package and priorities; this presentation change does not extend their authority
or automatically run Simulation.

## Purposeful package construction and shortlist quality

The large-package envelope is a capability limit, not a preference for large
transactions. Ranked strategic lanes now build bounded one- and two-asset
structures around their targets. Existing cardinal Market values and Decision's
existing economic tolerance locate a negotiating envelope; candidate order does
not minimize a Market gap or replace either team's improvement objectives.
Larger bundles grow only when the smaller structures cannot reach that envelope,
and stop growing when it is reached. Reverse construction supports acquiring or
spending pick bundles. At most 78 lightweight bundle entries for twelve
ingredients are indexed per direction; the Cartesian package-pair universe is
never materialized. At least one eighth of the visit budget remains exploratory, including
unusual large structures and missing-price assets. The eight-per-side / ten-total
limits, 640 total visits and eight preliminary screens remain unchanged.

Existing economic bands precede diversity in allocating full screens. Within
that boundary, joint priority-gain hypotheses can use published lineup membership,
position replacement floors and already published raw horizon deltas to identify
promising investigations. The lineup hint is deliberately optimistic, can overlap
slots, and is never reported as a competitive or utility result. Full roster,
lineup and mandatory-cut Decision checks determine the displayed consequences.
Complexity is a tie-breaker after the strategic hypothesis, not a universal score.

Economic strain or a dominated post-cut side keeps a package out of the main
shortlist. Other costly or incomplete candidates remain available for review and
Trade Center. Current comparisons use Decision's existing numerical tolerance.
When Current is requested, a purely future-ledger tradeoff with no Current change
needs further review instead of automatically leading the page because a future
component is positive. Explicit future-only directions remain supported. Current
upgrades with visible future sacrifices may still be displayed as Tradeoffs.

These rules do not change Value, Forecast, Shapley, Simulation, owner preferences
or final Decision. They do not assign an acceptance probability, combine horizon
units, or establish future team utility from additive asset contribution. Real
league validation must assess usefulness and sacrifices, not only size coverage
and route success.

## Timeline and positional transaction direction — 2026-11-15

Competitive state guides hypotheses; it does not prescribe every transaction.
A contender may exchange excess WR depth for future contribution or draft capital
while preserving competitive strength. Developing/rebuilding teams may prioritize
3-Year and Career benefits even without Current gains. Explicit user directions
take precedence over default inference, and multiple selected goals remain
independent channels.

Transaction-level position context must consider optimized starter/FLEX/Superflex
roles, incoming replacements, remaining forecasted depth, mandatory cuts and
resilience for both teams. Player counts and competitive labels alone do not
establish surplus. Selling depth can have a real injury/bye coverage cost even
when the projected starting lineup is unchanged. Keep those costs visible.

The implementation sequence is: expose trustworthy native/cut-adjusted evidence
and positional consequences; add symmetric directional strategy interpretation;
settle separate native materiality and final Decision integration; consolidate
Trade Center presentation; then enrich owner intelligence and discovery.
Directional evidence must not silently become final action authority. New
materiality thresholds or cross-timeline exchange rates require their own
versioned evidence and review; Market/display scales do not supply them.

## Purpose-driven discovery and additive owner evidence — 2026-11-26

Competitive outlook identifies potentially valuable improvements; roster construction
identifies ways to pursue them and their costs. Owner history adds partners and
package ideas. It must not eliminate ordinary paths, override explicit directions,
rewrite a team's competitive state, or convert an observed acquisition into a claim
that an owner will accept another one.

Construct packages around source-bound purposes before evaluating them: a position
upgrade, testing projected depth for draft capital, or acquiring younger/future
assets. A contender can investigate depth-for-picks exchanges; a rebuild label does
not forbid a player acquisition. Bench membership is a hypothesis about supply,
not proof of surplus or replaceability. Same-State published lineups and positional
strengths are required for lineup/depth purposes; missing evidence preserves the
ordinary paths without creating a claim.

Admitted, dated position acquisitions and receipt-backed pick acquisitions can add
specific ingredient pools outside ordinary prefixes. These are descriptive positive
examples, not learned preference scores. Missing history supplies no negative
signal. Do not infer an age-at-trade or historical youth preference from today's
player age. Current younger-player exploration and explicit age filters are separate.
The stronger contextual/residual policy still needs its existing validation gates.

Within existing guided-generation bounds, rotate two ordinary turns, one team-purpose
turn and one owner-history turn while each has work. Empty paths yield unused turns.
This is compute allocation, not a valuation weight. Preserve the existing exploration
reserve, ingredient/package/visit/screen limits and economic/priority gates. Finite
budgets cannot guarantee every previous package is retained; ordinary lane inventories
and access remain intact, with explicit coverage diagnostics. Among otherwise
comparable economically plausible packages, explore unvisited paths as well as
partners and outgoing assets.

Attach the purpose to each generated exchange and report what the unchanged
bilateral screen actually supports. A preliminary asset-contribution gain is not
optimized future franchise utility. A preserved current lineup is not confirmed
injury resilience. Owner evidence remains a factual reason to explore; confirmed
mutual benefit and acceptance remain separate questions.
