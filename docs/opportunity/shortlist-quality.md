# Search quality: compensate, compare, and curate

A positive coordinate does not establish that a costly trade is worth pursuing.
Keep Decision's directional evidence and final authority intact. Search may test
better terms and compare available alternatives; it may not invent a price at
which Current gains pay for losses in native 3-Year or Career contribution.

## Package improvement

Within the existing visit/screen budgets, a screened requested native loss can
seed a compensation path. Add one or two already-owned players/picks, retain the
original assets, respect the existing Market negotiating envelope before full
screen allocation, and run each child through normal legality, mandatory cuts,
lineup, Market and both-owner native-horizon screens. Use actual child effects,
including cuts, for a second step. This is a bounded negotiating hypothesis, not
an automatic entitlement to the other owner's picks. No child gains action
authority without the existing Decision evidence. Existing player substitution
repairs remain available. Each batch can attempt a path; prior screened IDs
prevent repeat evaluations. Explicit required offers, targets, age/position/type
filters and protected assets remain binding.

Generation favors packages without known requested native losses where otherwise
comparable. Rotate through different focal exchange families before repeating an
equivalent distant-pick exchange with another owner. Only identical released
neutral future-pick references share a family; actual projected slots remain
distinct. Owner history continues adding routes, without replacing these checks.

## Shortlist and alternatives

A mixed selected-priority vector remains available for closer review. It does
not automatically become a lead suggestion because one coordinate is positive.
Focused future directions can still match while sacrificing Current production;
all unrequested costs remain visible. This does not require every horizon to
improve and does not turn inferred competitive state into a permanent owner rule.

Among priority-compatible evaluated packages, compare focal Current weekly points,
3-Year contribution, Career contribution and Market capital separately. Prefer
an alternative only when it is at least as good in every complete coordinate and
better in at least one. Counterparty compatibility is a constraint, not a goal of
maximizing how much value we give away. Missing channels do not become zeros.
Fold identical evaluated focal effects into linked alternatives while retaining
each counterparty's own effects and required cuts. These comparisons grant no
final recommendation, acceptance estimate or claim of future franchise utility.

Small signed continuation receipts retain only fit IDs, four numeric coordinates
and family identity, bound to the existing exact input/tenant scope. They keep
counts, alternative folding and new-batch comparisons coherent without retaining
State/model graphs, rerunning prior screens, or adding persistence. Existing
80-screen, 4-entry/4-MiB response cache and request-token bounds remain in force.

## Consumer experience and limits

Lead with matching packages. Keep unresolved tradeoffs accessible in a closed
review section. Say "More QB points now" rather than declaring an overall QB
upgrade. Generic acquisition counts are not explanations for an individual deal;
contextual history remains available in team details. A no-match result is valid.

The delivery does not recalibrate native future forecasts or Career tails. A
backup QB's long-tail economics can require separate model evidence even when
arithmetic and source identity reconcile. Search tests and synthetic repaired
packages do not certify real-league usefulness or physical-device acceptance.
