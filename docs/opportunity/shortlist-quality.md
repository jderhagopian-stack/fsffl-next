## Funded terms and position anchors

An in-range singleton does not prove that funded terms are unnecessary. Guided
search may emit one player+two later-picks alternative per target/lane using
canonical pick rounds and the unchanged price envelope. A separate bounded
term window uses at most12 ingredients (up to4 later picks), at most48 scalar
price checks per anchor; emitted alternatives consume existing generation visits.
It does not increase the80 screens,8 confirmations, global visit limit or memory
reserve. The existing four-total-assets direction turn can investigate an
admissible funded alternative; no shape overrides economics or user constraints.

Position searches preserve ordinary player lanes. Up to2 matching player anchors
per partner get dedicated sweetener lanes with a required incoming anchor, so
picks cannot become a stand-in for the requested position. Explicit player-only
excludes sweeteners. Explicit pick-only retains its prior behavior: a position
selector does not impose an NFL position on a draft pick. Age constraints apply
to player anchors. Repairs use the same whole-package matching contract.

Coverage is not promotion. Required cuts, legal IR plans, requested priorities,
native availability, competitive confirmation and alternative comparisons retain
authority. Publish paired actual packages and displaced screens, including weak
or unconfirmed candidates. An extra pair of picks is not inherently a good deal.

# Search quality: compensate, compare, and curate

A positive coordinate does not establish that a costly trade is worth pursuing.
Keep Decision's directional evidence and final authority intact. Search may test
better terms and compare available alternatives; it may not invent a price at
which Current gains pay for losses in native 3-Year or Career contribution.

## Package improvement

Within the existing visit/screen budgets, a screened requested native loss can
seed a compensation path. Add one or two already-owned players/picks, retain the
original assets, respect the existing Market negotiating envelope before full
screen allocation, and run each child through normal legality, mandatory cuts,
lineup, Market and both-owner native-horizon screens. Use actual child effects,
including cuts, for a second step. This is a bounded negotiating hypothesis, not
an automatic entitlement to the other owner's picks. No child gains action
authority without the existing Decision evidence. Existing player substitution
repairs remain available. Each batch can attempt a path; prior screened IDs
prevent repeat evaluations. Explicit required offers, targets, age/position/type
filters and protected assets remain binding.

Generation favors packages without known requested native losses where otherwise
comparable. Rotate through different focal exchange families before repeating an
equivalent distant-pick exchange with another owner. Only identical released
neutral future-pick references share a family; actual projected slots remain
distinct. Owner history continues adding routes, without replacing these checks.

## Shortlist and alternatives

A mixed selected-priority vector remains available for closer review. It does
not automatically become a lead suggestion because one coordinate is positive.
Focused future directions can still match while sacrificing Current production;
all unrequested costs remain visible. This does not require every horizon to
improve and does not turn inferred competitive state into a permanent owner rule.

Among priority-compatible evaluated packages, compare native horizons and Market
separately. Current weekly lineup points are only a preliminary approximation.
When both exact-input full checks exist, use authoritative expected wins, playoff,
first-place and championship effects plus resilience. Never compare a full vector
to a weekly proxy, replace missing evidence with zero, or let the proxy overrule
the full result. Different input identities remain incomparable.

Existing Decision materiality can establish comparable competitive benefit when
the per-candidate Decision bands agree AND pairwise differences are immaterial.
Crossing a material-loss/gain boundary is not erased by a small pairwise delta.
Within that existing indifference, less future sacrifice can make an admissible
alternative preferable; outside it, preserve the independent Pareto choices.
Market indifference also remains the existing upstream policy. No universal
Current/future weighting, new player/pick value or future utility is introduced.

Equivalent focal exchanges can be linked, with each partner's own cuts/effects.
Different payments for the same acquisition remain visible when their timeline
consequences are genuinely incomparable; a Current-first lexicographic sort is
not a reason to discard them. Inferred goal sets receive stable rotated coverage
independent of their tuple order. Explicit instructions remain binding.

Screening and confirmation are separate finite work queues. Signed exact-input
receipts retain enough package/effect/source identity for a confirmation-only
batch after the screening pool or80-screen allowance is exhausted. No enumeration
or rescreening occurs there. At most one full check/batch and eight attempts/search
(including deferrals) prevent repeated resource-admission retries. Completed-empty,
still-checking and unable-to-finish are distinct states. Existing4-entry/4MiB cache,
expiry, tenant scope, memory reserve and exact scenario reuse remain controlling.

A zero-lineup-change contender bench conversion may enter investigation through
Decision-owned optimized role/future evidence. That preliminary hypothesis does
not prove surplus or preserved resilience. Full competitive, role, lineup,
coverage, resilience, future-loss, cut and protected-asset checks still own promotion.
Explicit Current-only instructions do not silently become future instructions.

## Consumer experience and limits

Lead with matching packages. Keep unresolved tradeoffs accessible in a closed
review section. Say "More QB points now" rather than declaring an overall QB
upgrade. Generic acquisition counts are not explanations for an individual deal;
contextual history remains available in team details. A no-match result is valid.

The delivery does not recalibrate native future forecasts or Career tails. A
backup QB's long-tail economics can require separate model evidence even when
arithmetic and source identity reconcile. Search tests and synthetic repaired
packages do not certify real-league usefulness or physical-device acceptance.
