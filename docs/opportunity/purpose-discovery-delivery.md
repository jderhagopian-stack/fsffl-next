# Purpose and owner-expansion delivery

The existing source/State/Forecast/Value/Utility/Decision pipeline is preserved.
This delivery extends the existing strategic package lanes and lazy generator.
No new model fit, universal value, preference multiplier, persistence system,
service or paid dependency is introduced.

## Delivered paths

- Position-upgrade targets use matching published lineups, the descriptive
  league-average position comparison, and governed current forecasts. Targets
  are pinned into generated packages; this is not a label attached afterward.
- Depth-to-capital paths test projected bench players who could improve a
  partner's current positional lineup. The tested player is pinned into the
  outgoing package. Weekly replacement, cuts, price and objectives still decide
  the actual effects; surplus is never assumed.
- Future-asset paths explore current players below their 27th birthday and picks
  alongside the unchanged native-horizon paths. Unknown age excludes only the
  younger-player path, not ordinary exploration. No age premium is added.
- Positive observed acquisitions can introduce pick- or position-led ingredient
  lists for either franchise regardless of its inferred competitive label.
  Qualified archive examples and source-observed current receipts are fenced
  by owner/family/cutoff. Current undated position labels and generic aggregate
  position counts do not become historical position proof. No historical
  youth preference is activated without dated age evidence.

User targets, offered assets, types, age/position constraints, partners and protected
players remain binding. Ordinary inventories remain unchanged. Guided allocation
reserves ordinary access and adds bounded purpose/history access; empty paths lend
their turns to available work. Duplicate canonical exchanges merge provenance and
still receive one counted screen. The existing exploration reserve remains.

## Product and acceptance

Cards briefly explain the reason for exploring the exchange and relevant recorded
history. Preliminary results distinguish a lineup gain, current-lineup preservation,
asset-contribution gain and an unconfirmed purpose. They do not alter Decision
classification, materiality or final Trade Center disposition.

Acceptance includes an owner-history positive control that adds an asset beyond
ordinary ingredient limits; no-history and stale/foreign-evidence controls; unchanged
ordinary inventories; explicit directions/protections; ordinary/purpose/history
budget accounting; 1-QB and Superflex fixture optimizers and native trade screens;
no changed-state Simulation calls; real source-case coverage where retained;
mobile/desktop card behavior and bounded memory/latency checks.

Fixture success and retained historical evidence do not certify actual live-trade
usefulness. Compare normal same-State searches and their package provenance and
priority-gate receipts before expanding budgets or promoting a learned owner policy.
