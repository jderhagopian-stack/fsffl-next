# Market Focus authority boundary

Market Focus is Search intent, not analytical truth.

The server applies the selected strategic posture and explicit task before the product candidate limit. Position, target and consolidation focus narrow the full structural candidate catalog. Shopping a player rebuilds package neighborhoods constrained to include that player. Strategic posture changes Search ordering only.

Market Focus does not alter canonical league State, FSFFL Value, calculated competitive state, Simulation, Trade Decision, materiality, owner acceptance uncertainty, or recommendation authority.
