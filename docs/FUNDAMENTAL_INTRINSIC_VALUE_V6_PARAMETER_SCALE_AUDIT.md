# Fundamental Intrinsic Value v6 — Parameter and Scale Audit

This bounded finishing audit classifies the remaining v6 inputs and tests the true modeling assumptions plus the customer display policy without reopening the Intrinsic architecture.

## Parameter authority

Empirically fitted: post-Year-3 continuation slopes; residual draft-pedigree model and coefficient; Forecast-owned non-QB career-transition evidence.

Rule/formula derived: league-wide starter demand from canonical lineup rules; FLEX/SUPERFLEX eligibility; effective positional supply from production concentration; relative starter pressure.

Policy defined: the 0.85 annual future-production discount; the discrete Intrinsic continuation age-band structure.

Presentation only: the monotonic 0–10,000 translation of completed economic Intrinsic raw.

## Future discount audit

The 0.85 discount is inherited policy, not an empirically identified price coefficient. A bounded 0.80 / 0.85 / 0.90 chronological sensitivity test shows 0.80 is easier to forecast, but football outcomes alone cannot identify an economically true time-preference rate because changing the rate changes both the prediction target and magnitude. That is insufficient evidence to redefine dynasty time preference. The 0.85 policy remains frozen in v6.

## Continuation age-boundary audit

The current Intrinsic continuation structure uses <=23, 24–26, 27–29 and 30+ bands, aligned to governed Forecast age cells. Adjacent breakpoint challengers moved aggregate chronological error only a few tenths of one percent in either direction; a simple continuous age formulation was worse in every fold. No alternative met the promotion standard, so the current buckets remain.

## Reference-universe audit

The former display policy used the top 216 historical economic assets per season. Its raw quantiles move materially as roster capacity changes, so 216 no longer defines the universal customer scale.

A league-specific dynasty-reference cohort remains a diagnostic and is derived from canonical league rules as `team_count x active roster_size`. It does not set the display conversion.

## Final customer scale

The final display is a simple concave economic-magnitude translation rather than a percentile leaderboard:

- 0 economic raw maps to 0.
- The all-positive production-parity PIT economic-raw p99 (`1540.989083049095`) maps to 9,500.
- Below that anchor: `9500 * sqrt(economic_raw / apex_raw)`.
- Above it: an asymptotic tail approaches 10,000.

The square-root transform is a bounded presentation choice for the heavy right-tailed economic coordinate. It uses one empirically derived scale anchor rather than assigning customer values to a series of historical percentiles. On the all-positive PIT raw distribution it naturally places approximately p20 at 1,200, p50 at 2,400, p75 at 3,600, p90 at 5,000, p95 at 6,800, p97 at 8,000 and p99 at 9,500.

This avoids the two audited presentation problems: roster-quantile mapping made ordinary assets too rich, while a purely linear p99 scale made strong non-QB assets implausibly small because the economic raw has a long right tail.

The display uses no fixed league roster count, current-player quantile fitting, named-player fitting, Broad Market, League Market or trade prices. It does not change raw economic ordering, Forecast, continuation, pedigree residuals or structural positional factors.

## Independence

The audit and calibration contracts record no Broad Market, League Market, Team Utility, owner-behavior, replacement-surplus or transaction-price inputs.
