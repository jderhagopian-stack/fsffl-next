# Fundamental Intrinsic Value v6 — Parameter and Scale Audit

This bounded finishing audit classifies the remaining v6 inputs and tests the two true modeling assumptions plus the customer display policy without reopening the Intrinsic architecture.

## Parameter authority

### Empirically fitted

- post-Year-3 continuation slopes;
- residual draft-pedigree model and coefficient;
- Forecast-owned non-QB career-transition evidence.

### Rule / formula derived

- league-wide starter demand from canonical lineup rules;
- FLEX and SUPERFLEX eligibility;
- effective positional supply from production concentration;
- relative starter pressure used for structural positional economics.

### Policy defined

- the 0.85 annual future-production discount;
- the discrete Intrinsic continuation age-band structure.

### Presentation only

- the monotonic 0–10,000 translation of completed economic Intrinsic raw.

## Future discount audit

The 0.85 discount is inherited policy, not an empirically identified price coefficient. Football outcomes alone cannot identify an economically true time-preference rate because changing the rate changes both the prediction target and the economic magnitude.

A bounded 0.80 / 0.85 / 0.90 chronological sensitivity test was therefore treated as a predictability/stability diagnostic rather than a parameter-fitting exercise. 0.80 has lower normalized forecast error than 0.85 and 0.90, but that result is expected to favor stronger discounting of harder-to-predict distant production and is not sufficient evidence to redefine dynasty time preference. The 0.85 policy remains frozen in v6.

## Continuation age-boundary audit

The current Intrinsic continuation structure uses <=23, 24–26, 27–29 and 30+ bands. Those boundaries align to the governed Forecast age cells, whose underlying transition evidence is separately estimated.

Adjacent breakpoint challengers on chronological holdouts moved the aggregate error only a few tenths of one percent in either direction; a simple continuous age formulation was worse in every fold. No alternative met the promotion standard. The current buckets remain frozen in v6 and are documented as an evidence-aligned modeling structure rather than uniquely optimal cut points.

## Reference-universe audit

The former display policy used the top 216 historical economic assets per season (12 teams x 18 active roster spots). Sensitivity testing showed that its raw quantiles move materially when roster capacity changes. Therefore 216 is no longer allowed to define the universal customer scale.

A league-specific dynasty-reference cohort remains useful diagnostically and is derived from canonical league rules as:

`team_count x active roster_size`

It does not set the display conversion.

## Final customer scale

The final display is cardinal rather than percentile-semantic:

- 0 economic raw -> 0;
- the all-positive production-parity PIT economic-raw p99 (`1540.989083049095`) -> 9,500;
- values below that anchor scale linearly, preserving economic magnitude ratios;
- values above it compress asymptotically toward 10,000.

The p99 anchor is based on the stable all-positive PIT economic distribution and does not use a fixed league roster count, current-player quantiles, named players, Broad Market, League Market or trade prices.

This scale is presentation-only. It does not change raw economic ordering, Forecast, continuation, pedigree residuals or structural positional factors.

## Independence

The audit and calibration contracts record no Broad Market, League Market, Team Utility, owner-behavior, replacement-surplus or transaction-price inputs.
