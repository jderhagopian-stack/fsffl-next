# League Atlas Plan — Authorized Only After Reference-System Review

## Owner question

**What does the competitive landscape of this league look like?**

The answer must be visually discoverable before the user reads a table.

## First viewport

1. League identity and current competitive shape.
2. One atlas field showing the most structurally important dimension at a glance.
3. Managed-franchise location inside that landscape.
4. One short “where leverage sits” interpretation.
5. One route into Market when governed opportunity context exists.

## Atlas layers

### Positional power
- Rank/strength Atlas Cells for QB/RB/WR/TE;
- league-average benchmark;
- managed team and extremes visually distinct;
- tap team or position to focus.

### Competitive state
- contender / competitive / developing / rebuilding structure from Team Utility;
- visually separate from owner posture.

### Age / duration
- Trajectory/age bands from canonical player age only;
- no guessed ages.

### Draft capital
- governed pick inventory and Cardinal Value kept distinct;
- trajectory across draft seasons.

### Fragility / depth
- Risk Band for largest single-player lineup drop and governed bench coverage;
- no invented risk thresholds.

### Complementary relationships
- only where evidence supports the relationship;
- use supply/need discovery and Market focus;
- no presentation-created trade recommendation.

## Interaction

SEE:
- atlas exposes monopolies, shortages and structural clusters.

UNDERSTAND:
- exact ranks/indices appear on hover/tap.

INTERACT:
- select a team, position or dimension; atlas re-focuses without changing analytical truth.

DRILL:
- open exact rows, evidence/provenance or Market exploration.

## Mobile

- no compressed full-width matrix as the default;
- one dimension at a time with swipe/tap selector;
- managed team pinned;
- top/bottom extremes summarized visually;
- exact league table remains drill-down.

## Implementation gate

Do not implement this plan until management accepts the reference visual-system candidate as materially closer to the North Star. Do not merely color the current League rows.
