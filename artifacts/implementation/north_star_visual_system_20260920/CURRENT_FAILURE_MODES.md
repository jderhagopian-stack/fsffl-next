# Current Visual-System Failure Modes — 2026-09-20

This diagnosis uses management's direct live-beta observation as current rendered-product evidence, prior rendered beta review, and current repository-authoritative UI composition. Authenticated live pixel inspection is still unavailable from this standard chat.

## Recurring system-level problems

1. **Card/panel repetition flattens hierarchy**
   Too many sections are expressed as similar dark rounded boxes. Grouping and importance become visually equivalent.

2. **Primary anchors are not strong enough across the product**
   Franchise is closest, but other surfaces often start with headings and panels rather than one unmistakable visual truth.

3. **Too much information lives at the same scale**
   Small labels, modest numbers and explanatory paragraphs compete rather than establishing a scan order.

4. **Identity is underpowered**
   Player, franchise and owner names appear, but often as text inside generic UI rather than as the organizing object of the page.

5. **Prose and numbers do too much work**
   League, Market and Trade frequently require reading to discover relationships that could be visually encoded.

6. **Graphical storytelling is local rather than systemic**
   Useful bars and matrices exist, but there is no shared mature grammar for rank, delta, probability, risk, trajectory, distribution and atlas structure.

7. **Dense dark surfaces reduce visual contrast between importance levels**
   The current palette can remain dark, but major surfaces need more open space, larger typographic contrast and fewer nested containers.

8. **Primary intelligence and evidence are not separated consistently**
   Several routes have good details drawers, but the overall product still mixes primary interpretation, exact metrics and methods too close together.

9. **Mobile still inherits desktop density in important areas**
   Several screens have responsive rules, but the recurring product pattern is still “compress the desktop construct” rather than deliberately reorder the story.

10. **Cross-surface language is inconsistent**
    Home, Franchise, League, Market, Trade and Owner each have custom local patterns. Good ideas do not yet add up to one product language.

## Strongest existing patterns to preserve

- Franchise hero + position bands + separated roster/assets views;
- progressive drill-down and fail-closed unavailable states;
- Market/Trade workflow handoffs;
- exact numeric evidence alongside presentation;
- newly accepted semantic Delta Band from PR #159;
- Owner scan-first shift from PR #158.

## Conclusion

The corrective target is not “lighter cards” or “bigger bars.” It is a system that changes the default reading behavior from **read the dashboard** to **see the truth, understand the relationship, then choose what to inspect**.
