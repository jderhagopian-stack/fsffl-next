# FSFFL NEXT — Production-data / commercial-source readiness matrix

Status: management gate artifact for PR #147. This is an implementation/readiness classification, not a legal opinion or a commercial-use approval.

## Governing architecture

The production boundary is **provider/source → canonical FSFFL facts → Forecast → Shapley Intrinsic**. I1 consumes provider-neutral canonical facts. Provider-specific status codes, field names, licensing metadata, and transformation details must remain inside adapters/provenance. A provider can be replaced without changing I1 meaning, Shapley meaning, or the Shapley-native API contract, provided the replacement maps to the same canonical facts and passes parity/coverage gates.

Missing source coverage is explicit. It must never be silently interpreted as healthy, active, retained, released, retired, injured, or non-persistent. Where the frozen reduced I1 path is valid, missing rich evidence degrades coverage rather than inventing facts. Where a required completed-source fact bundle or Year-1 governed Forecast is absent, Intrinsic is unavailable and fails closed.

## Private-beta versus commercial-launch disposition

No source in the project evidence is identified as **known to prohibit** the current intended private-beta use. Accordingly, unresolved commercialization rights are **not by themselves a private-beta blocker**. They remain explicit launch dependencies. The operational beta gate is instead: can the product supply the required provider-neutral canonical facts at sufficient coverage, or fall back/degrade exactly as the frozen I1 contract permits, without inventing facts?

At present, Year-1 projection evidence and current league identity are live. The completed-source I1 fact artifact is provider-neutral and validated but is not yet attached to the product runtime; rich roster/injury/participation feeds are also not live-wired. Those are activation/integration gaps, not reasons to contaminate I1 with provider semantics. The new Shapley-native endpoint must therefore expose `unavailable` until an authoritative completed-source bundle is attached, and may expose `degraded` when validated reduced-path evidence is available.

Commercial launch should require commercially suitable or appropriately licensed replacements/clearance for every source family still marked beta-only or unresolved, with canonical parity proving that provider substitution did not change Forecast meaning or Value semantics.
