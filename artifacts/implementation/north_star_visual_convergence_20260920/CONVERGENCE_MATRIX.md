## Closest / farthest

**Closest: Franchise.** It has the strongest combination of dominant diagnosis, ranked position bands, actionable pressure point, separated roster/assets subviews and progressive evidence.

**Farthest visually among high-value decision surfaces: Trade Center.** The governed workflow is rich, but the most important changed-roster evidence is communicated as signed numbers plus prose. The user should be able to see the direction and material size of the consequences before reading the explanation.

**League is the next largest visual-compression gap.** The data is structurally useful, but the atlas still relies heavily on matrices, rows and text blocks.

## Cross-surface conclusion

The application has made meaningful information-architecture progress. The remaining gap is now less “missing pages” and more “missing visual language.” Repeating surface-specific restyles would compound inconsistency. The next slices should establish shared comparison, identity, trajectory, risk and evidence primitives, then apply them to the highest-value workflows.
