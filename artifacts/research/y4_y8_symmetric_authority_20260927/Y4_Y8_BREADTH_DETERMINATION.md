# Y4-Y8 Model-Family Breadth Determination

Date: 2026-09-27  
Authority: **Research only / no production change**

## Determination

**The preserved Y4-Y8 family breadth is sufficient for this authority study. No additional model-family challenge is justified.**

This conclusion does not mean the existing models resolve every position×horizon cell. It means the remaining ambiguity is not plausibly explained by an obvious missing broad representational family.

## Families already represented

The frozen long-horizon program already contains, on chronological PIT coordinates:

- direct ridge / shrinkage models;
- two-part survival × conditional-production ridge;
- direct HistGradientBoosting nonlinear learners;
- two-part HistGradientBoosting;
- state / career-transition representations;
- global-continuous, position-continuous, shared-horizon and specialist architectures;
- trajectory-rich, football-history, pedigree and uncertainty feature sets;
- explicit nonlinear target-age / experience / cumulative-exposure variants;
- hard routing, soft stacking and fixed blended policies.

This is materially broader than the pre-challenge Y2/Y3 family universe that triggered the N1/N2 bounded challenge.

## Why another challenge is not warranted

The unresolved Y4-Y7 cells show:
- small / unstable differences between already-diverse models or policies;
- explicit MAE-versus-RMSE/tail tradeoffs;
- chronology sensitivity;
- survival-versus-conditional-production tradeoffs.

Y8's main limitation is **independent historical support**: only two valid repeated outer origins remain under the frozen chronology. Adding another learner cannot create missing outer evidence.

The age/exposure audit likewise shows that richer nonlinear representation can help selected components/cells, but no stable residual pattern demonstrates that the candidate universe is unable to express the relevant structure.

Therefore a further family search would be more likely to create model shopping than resolve authority.

## Consequence

Continue with the existing candidate universe and represent unresolved cells as:
- tied;
- tradeoff;
- uncertainty-forward;
- or evidence-limited.

Do not add another model family in this Y4-Y8 directive.

Production H3, Forecast and Intrinsic remain unchanged.
