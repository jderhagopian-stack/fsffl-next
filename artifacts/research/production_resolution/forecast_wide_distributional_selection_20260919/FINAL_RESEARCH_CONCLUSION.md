## Final classification

**C. FORECAST SELECTION METHODOLOGY REVISION EARNED**

The elite-RB issue is not only an RB special case. Applying one preregistered, position-agnostic proper-score framework to the frozen D0/D1 candidates identifies a second independent point-vs-distribution disagreement at QB.

- QB: **DISTRIBUTIONAL ALTERNATIVE EARNED** for Y3 developmental, D0 -> D1. D1 lowers raw-point CRPS in early, mid, and validation; the pooled 2014-2020 CRPS improvement is significant, the MAE guardrail does not show significant degradation, and 2021-2022 preserves the CRPS direction with no significant MAE regression.
- RB: **DISTRIBUTIONAL ALTERNATIVE EARNED** for Y3 established, D0 -> D1. This reproduces the prior RB methodology concern and survives the same frozen selection and locked replication standard.
- WR: **KEEP CURRENT P0 REPRESENTATION**. The supported D1 cells are favored by proper-score evidence; the supported veteran D0 cell does not earn a D1 switch because CRPS evidence is not significant and point MAE worsens.
- TE: **KEEP CURRENT P0 REPRESENTATION**. Supported D1 cells are favored; veteran Y3 remains too weakly supported to earn any route change.

This means proper distributional scoring has earned a place in general Forecast candidate-selection/validation authority, with ordinary point MAE retained as a guardrail. It does **not** mean every position changes route, and it does not authorize production implementation.

The earned research changes are narrow: Y3 developmental QB D0 -> D1 and Y3 established RB D0 -> D1 under the preregistered proper-score standard. WR and TE remain controls demonstrating that the method can preserve P0 when the evidence supports it.
