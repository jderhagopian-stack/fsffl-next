# Recommended next research boundary

## Decision
Do not promote C1 or C2. Retain frozen D1 for Y3 developmental RB.

## What this stage learned
The threshold-free mathematical direction was stable in one narrow sense: both candidates learned a slightly larger multiplier at high source percentile than at the middle in all nine origins. But the fitted common location factor was below 1 everywhere, so it globally shrank active-production support and made the known high-source underprediction worse.

The failure is not evidence for a p90/p95 state. It is evidence that a single common multiplier across all positive future states is too blunt.

In the frozen p90+/p95+ rows, realized-state diagnostics show D1 underpredicts elite/premium conditional production while overpredicting starter/usable (and sparse depth) conditional production. Those opposite signs cannot be corrected safely by one row-level multiplier applied to every positive state.

## Next bounded research question
Before fitting another Forecast candidate, test whether state-specific continuous-magnitude calibration is identifiable with adequate origin support and stable regularization. If identifiable, a later directive may preregister a low-complexity hierarchical/shared-curvature calibration of the five D1 positive-state conditional means. It must:
- remain threshold-free;
- keep D1 state probabilities frozen unless a separate directive earns reopening them;
- use the canonical 2014-2022 Y3 walk-forward coordinate;
- retain proper scoring and MAE/no-harm gates;
- preserve source-2023 -> target-2026 as prospective confirmation;
- avoid downstream double counting.

Do not reuse p90/p95 as model states, and do not tune a state-specific candidate in this task.
