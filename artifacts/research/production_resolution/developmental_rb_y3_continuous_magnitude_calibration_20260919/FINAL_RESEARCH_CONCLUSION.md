# Final research conclusion

## Classification
C. CONDITIONAL-PRODUCTION CALIBRATION DOES NOT RESOLVE THE PROBLEM

## Result
The two preregistered threshold-free, low-complexity location calibrations (C1 bounded affine and C2 bounded quadratic) do not earn research authority.

Both candidates preserve D1 state probabilities exactly and define coherent six-state Forecast distributions. Both improve raw-point MAE by about two points, and both learn a stable relative shape in which the high-magnitude multiplier is slightly larger than the middle-magnitude multiplier in all nine origins.

That is not enough. The fitted calibration is a global shrinkage: the multiplier is below 1 across the entire source-magnitude range. As a result:
- pooled full-cell CRPS worsens versus D1;
- only 4 of 9 origins improve;
- every leave-one-origin-out pooled result remains negative;
- integrated continuous-magnitude calibration error worsens;
- p75+, p90+, and p95+ active-retention compression all worsen materially;
- predictive interval coverage narrows and declines.

C2 is slightly less harmful than C1, but it does not satisfy any authority gate that C1 fails.

## Why the common location factor fails
At high source magnitude, D1 conditional-production errors differ by realized positive future state. In p90+ rows, elite and premium state means are modestly underpredicted while starter/usable state means are overpredicted. A single multiplier applied to all five positive states cannot correct both directions simultaneously.

This is a conditional-production representation problem, not evidence for a hard p90/p95 state and not evidence that the D1 route or state-probability layer should be reopened here.

## Cross-position diagnostic
Applying the richer C2 shape diagnostically to adequately supported Y3 developmental/established QB, WR, and TE routes yields negative pooled CRPS gain in all six tested routes. The common multiplicative location form therefore does not appear to be a generally useful Forecast calibration shape.

## Authority
D1 remains the frozen Y3 developmental-RB control. No production authority changes. Source-2023 -> target-2026 remains untouched for prospective confirmation.

## Next bounded research boundary
Before fitting another candidate, test identifiability of state-specific threshold-free continuous-magnitude calibration with shared/hierarchical regularization across D1 positive future states. The purpose would be to allow premium/elite and starter/usable conditional production to respond differently to magnitude while preserving frozen D1 state probabilities. This requires a new directive and preregistration.
