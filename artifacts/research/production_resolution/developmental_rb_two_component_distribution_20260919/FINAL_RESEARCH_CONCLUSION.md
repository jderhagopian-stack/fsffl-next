# FSFFL NEXT - Developmental RB two-component active-production research conclusion

## Final classification

C. TWO-COMPONENT REPRESENTATION NOT SUPPORTED

## Plain-language result

The preregistered M1 active-production mixture does not earn research authority. In frozen 2014-2020 p95+ Y3 cases it improves the combined survivor/downside calibration error relative to D1 by about 0.111, showing why a survivor/decline decomposition is intuitively attractive. But that improvement is purchased with materially worse raw-point CRPS, severe expected-point MAE degradation, harm below p90, and a failed Y2 no-harm control.

The reason is visible in the fitted mixture itself. The higher-log-retention component is extremely broad, so its lognormal mean implies implausibly large expected production. At the same time, the fitted survivor-component probability falls as source percentile rises (negative beta1 in every chronology fit), meaning the latent components are not recovering the intended exceptional-source survivor path. They are largely separating extreme retention-ratio outcomes, including low-source breakouts, rather than creating a stable high-source survivor probability.

Locked 2021-2022 does not rescue the structure. Y3 CRPS remains worse than D1 by about 0.913 points on average, MAE worsens by about 13.8 points, and p95+ combined tail calibration is worse than D1 by about 0.028. The locked sample is small and is validation only, but it points against promotion rather than toward it.

## Architecture conclusion

The three-layer Forecast concept remains coherent in principle: D1 inactivity can remain separate from conditional active-production uncertainty, Simulation can sample once from Forecast, and Value should not add a second collapse haircut. This experiment does not establish that a two-component log-retention mixture is the right active-production representation.

M2 was not authorized because the immediately preceding governed identifiability audit found no stable additional point-in-time conditioning dimension. No current-player tuning, manual tail mass, hand-set component mean, tier, floor, or collapse probability was used.

## Authority boundary

P0/D1 remains the frozen control for Y3 developmental RB. No implementation, merge, deploy, or production-authority change is authorized. Any further distribution-family work requires a new management directive and fresh preregistration; locked 2021-2022 cannot be reused to tune the next candidate.
