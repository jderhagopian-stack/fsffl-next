# Final classification

**C. DISTRIBUTIONAL REPRESENTATION CONCERN - RESEARCH REQUIRED BEFORE MERGE**

## Plain-language conclusion

The evidence does not support treating the current elite-RB Year-3 shape as merely a conservative mean that can be accepted without further Forecast research.

P0 is reproducible and is not mechanically broken. It also already carries useful downside information through state probabilities, especially for developmental D1. The problem is narrower: the historical elite-RB outcome distribution is much wider and more state-dependent than the point-error objective used to choose/consume the conditional-production treatment.

Three findings drive the classification.

1. **High-source developmental RBs are systematically mean-reverted too aggressively.** In the frozen 2014-2020 developmental high-source band, active Year-3 realized retention had a median of 0.698; the frozen P0 conditional-active forecast median was 0.520. Mean P0-minus-realized bias was -0.126, with a bootstrap 95% interval [-0.242, -0.006]. Across all active developmental top-decile rows, source magnitude has essentially no relationship with realized retention (Spearman rho 0.028) but P0 imposes a very strong negative relationship (rho -0.832).

2. **Established D0 collapses historically distinct active outcomes into a middle point.** Frozen selection-window established top-decile active Y3 outcomes put only 4.5% of observations in the 0.40-0.60 retention band, while 63.6% were below 0.40 and 31.8% were at least 0.60. Current D0 established elite-RB conditional retentions cluster near that sparse middle region because all positive states share one production mean.

3. **The point-MAE route objective and distributional accuracy disagree.** For top-decile Y3 RBs, D1 beats D0 on CRPS in every frozen early/mid/validation block for both developmental and established cohorts, and again in 2021-2022. On the full established route cell, D0 had been kept because D1 did not win ordinary MAE in every chronology block. This is exactly the management concern: a point-error objective can prefer a central mean while a distributional objective prefers explicit survivor/downside separation.

The locked 2021-2022 replication strengthens, rather than weakens, the concern: top-decile RB active Y3 median retention reproduces at 0.731; developmental is 0.728. In the decision-loss proxy, underprediction/premature downgrade accounts for most Year-3 error mass in the post-freeze all-RB, developmental, and established cohorts.

## Important limitation

This diagnostic does **not** prove a mathematically bimodal population. One-vs-two-component Gaussian-mixture BIC prefers the simpler one-component description in the available samples. The concern is instead that a low conditional mean or D0 common active mean is a poor representation for Simulation/decision purposes when realized mass is concentrated in severe-downside and productive-survivor regions.

## Narrow next research layer

Keep the state-probability architecture and current production authority frozen. Before merge authorization, run one bounded Forecast-only experiment that evaluates conditional-production representations with a proper distributional scoring objective (CRPS and, where support permits, log score), while retaining ordinary point MAE as a guardrail rather than the sole selection authority. The experiment should test the already-governed D0/D1 state representation and bounded within-state uncertainty on the frozen 2014-2020 chronology, then evaluate once on locked 2021-2022. It must not use current-player names, create an RB premium/floor, change current routes, or touch Value/Shapley.

No production/model/routing change is made by this diagnostic.
