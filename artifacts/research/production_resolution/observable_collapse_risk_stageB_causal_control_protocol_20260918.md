## Causal families to classify independently
Each frozen high-leverage coordinate may receive one or more evidence-backed labels using only evidence dated no later than the source-season forecast cutoff:
- temporary/recoverable injury or availability
- persistent physical risk
- documented role / starting-job erosion
- roster / contract-security risk
- credible successor pressure already present
- age / retirement risk
- mixed structural risk
- future shock (material causal fact occurs only after cutoff)
- unresolved / insufficient contemporaneous evidence

The roster-status warning family (prolonged/repeated/late source-season non-active stretches) is evidence, not a prerequisite. Active players remain eligible for every structural family.

## Evidence standard
A label requires a dated contemporaneous source or a frozen structured source-time fact. Later reporting may corroborate chronology but cannot be backfilled as a source-time predictor. Speculation is not converted into fact.

## Matched controls
For each candidate family, non-collapse controls must be drawn from the same frozen historical population with matching/stratification on:
- position
- source season / chronology
- age or experience neighborhood
- source-production neighborhood / state where feasible
- roster-availability burden where relevant

A family cannot reach Stage-C eligibility merely because it is common in collapse cases. It must show discrimination versus matched controls and be reproducibly obtainable for current players at the same information cutoff.
