## Executive conclusion

The evidence supports a **term structure**, not a larger replacement master score.

Research recommends the following architecture for Management consideration:

1. **Near-term / H1** — expose the already-governed Year-1 Shapley contribution as the immediate-value view.
2. **Governed H3** — keep the current production `intrinsic-shapley-i1-v1` three-year coordinate unchanged.
3. **Long H5 challenger** — treat a five-year cumulative Shapley view as a distinct challenger coordinate. The historical Y4/Y5 extension adds useful chronological out-of-time rank signal beyond simply carrying Y3 forward.
4. **Terminal / career** — do **not** publish an exact Y6-Y8/career cardinal value from the present evidence. Terminal should be a coarse, uncertainty-forward persistence/career-state view until a separate terminal contract is validated.

Do not collapse these views into an unexplained master score.

## Uncertainty interpretation

Long-horizon uncertainty is substantial.

The historical OOT residual-RMSE floor at H5 is:
- QB **101.1** standard fantasy points;
- RB **52.4**;
- WR **35.4**;
- TE **30.4**.

Against current H5 mean expected production, those floors equal roughly:
- QB **2.0×** the mean;
- RB **2.4×**;
- WR **1.3×**;
- TE **1.8×**.

By H8, the monotone uncertainty floor is roughly **2.8× / 7.2× / 2.5× / 3.4×** current mean production for QB/RB/WR/TE.

Raw RMSE falls in later horizons partly because more players realize zero production. Research therefore keeps a **monotone non-decreasing uncertainty envelope** rather than treating lower raw RMSE as more certainty.

A precise cumulative H5/H8 Value standard deviation is not yet promoted because cross-horizon error covariance has not been validated. That is a required future gate.

## Discount / terminal sensitivity

Using 0.70, 0.85 and 0.95 discount factors changed absolute long-horizon error and magnitude materially while leaving rank ordering relatively stable.

At the historical 2021 H5 Shapley bridge, Spearman was approximately **0.648-0.650** across all three discounts, while MAE ranged from about **56.6 to 91.0**.

Therefore:
- this study does **not** claim 0.85 is newly optimized for H5/terminal value;
- 0.85 should remain the continuity/reference discount in a challenger because it is already governed at H3;
- any production H5 cardinal scale needs a separate magnitude/calibration decision rather than selecting a discount because rankings “look right.”

## Economic usefulness versus Forecast accuracy

A key negative result is important.

The Y4/Y5 model improves annual long-horizon Forecast and rank signal, but adding those years to one replacement-surplus total changes aggregate historical economic prediction only trivially versus H3 alone.

This is evidence **against** replacing H3 with one larger blended number.

The incremental value of the research is the time structure itself: identifying durability, horizon crossovers and positional/age shifts.

## Proposed architecture

Research recommends this candidate contract:

`Near-term H1 | Governed H3 | Challenger H5 | Terminal/Career band`

- **H1:** current contribution, already present inside the governed Shapley decomposition.
- **H3:** existing production authority, unchanged.
- **H5:** separate cumulative challenger using Y4/Y5 career-state production evidence, the same league deployment economy, explicit horizon uncertainty and no market inputs.
- **Terminal/Career:** non-cardinal persistence/career-state band; no exact discounted “career value” until separately validated.

Market, League Market, Owner Intelligence and Team Utility remain outside universal Intrinsic.

## What Research does not recommend

- replacing production H3 now;
- adding Y6-Y8 precise annual values to production;
- using a perpetuity/terminal multiplier;
- fitting a discount to produce intuitive player rankings;
- adding a youth bonus, QB premium, positional multiplier or named-player override;
- using Broad Market to stabilize long-horizon Intrinsic;
- collapsing H1/H3/H5/terminal into a hidden master score.

## Required Management decisions

1. **Architecture:** approve or reject the four-part horizon curve as the next governed design target.
2. **H5 next phase:** authorize a bounded H5 challenger validation package while H3 remains production authority.
3. **QB gate:** require a QB-specific Y4/Y5 career-state extension or equivalent repair before H5 can seek production authority.
4. **Terminal representation:** approve a band/state representation as the next research target rather than an exact cardinal career value.
5. **Uncertainty:** require joint/cross-horizon uncertainty calibration before any H5 cardinal production promotion.
6. **Product use:** if H5 is later promoted, authorize downstream surfaces to consume separate horizon coordinates without replacing Team Utility or creating acceptance probability.
