# Intrinsic Horizon Semantics — Management acceptance, 2026-10-17

This note governs PR #464, **both horizon displays**, without changing the accepted economic models.

**Projections:** present/current season, including completed production plus rest-of-season governed forecast where appropriate. Not replaced by Intrinsic.

**3-Year Intrinsic:** expected marginal lineup contribution over the shorter three-year decision horizon. Productive veterans can appropriately score well even with little career tail.

**Career Intrinsic:** expected marginal contribution over the entire remaining career. It can emphasize long-lived upside for young assets; veterans may score materially lower than their 3-Year score.

The two index coordinates each have a horizon-specific fixed raw-magnitude ruler on the shared visible 0–10,000 range. **Do not impose** Career score >= 3-Year score, cross-horizon score equality, cross-horizon score arithmetic, a position-adjusted percentile quota, or an age-related score boost. Ranking/percentile remains informational only. Real raw-model valuations determine the scores.
