- Publish percentile as descriptive context only.
- For each of 331 matched records, measure ordering concordance, relative position, and disagreement under current and shadow Intrinsic scores. **Never** equate raw market-index minus intrinsic-index to trade surplus.
- Separate rank disagreement from estimated economically comparable gap. A value-exchange bridge, if developed, must be separately calibrated, out-of-sample validated, and free of direct market imitation.
- Future Team Utility consumes player economic contributions and roster-specific marginal objectives, not arbitrary subtraction or summation of display indices.
