# Current Football-State H3 Validation Gates

Frozen before outcome scoring.

## Rich/update evidence

A future H2/H3 event-state updater may be recommended only when:
- the relevant event family has at least 30 resolved OOT rows overall and at least two chronological holdout seasons;
- event-rich or event-family evidence improves at least one of persistence Brier, six-state Brier, or anticipated-production MAE by >=2% versus its immediate parent information set;
- no other core metric degrades by >5%;
- no tested position with n>=30 degrades six-state Brier by >7%;
- the direction is not driven by one holdout season.

If these conditions do not clear, event evidence remains explanatory/provenance only or coarse.

## Injury durability

A temporary/non-reserve injury may create H2/H3 durable adjustment only if:
- full availability evidence improves OOT future-state/persistence metrics beyond roster/continuity evidence under the rule above; and
- weekly episode evidence does not show near-full post-return role and production recovery for the relevant supported cohort.

Otherwise injury impact is current-season availability/role only.

## Structural events

Retirement, release/cut, suspension/exempt, team transition and reattachment are evaluated separately. Sparse cohorts remain explicit states with conservative/coarse semantics rather than fitted penalties.

## Current Y1 provider capture

Current provider ROS changes are research evidence only. A healthy two-source ROS view may be interpreted as already incorporating current availability/role into Y1. It must not be combined with an additional event haircut on the same Y1 points.

## No production changes

All thresholds govern Research interpretation only. Production H3 remains unchanged.
