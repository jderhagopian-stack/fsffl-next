# Phase-3/4 Symmetric Authority Reinterpretation

Date: 2026-09-27  
Authority: Research only; no production change.

## What is comparable

The Phase-3/4 candidates were generated on the same 15,504-row historical coordinate and share literal H1/H2/H3 rows. The durable aggregate evidence therefore supports a symmetric re-read without treating the historically selected route or baseline as privileged.

The exact row-level checkpoints remain authoritative for the candidates that persisted them. This audit does **not** claim new pairwise confidence intervals for Phase-3/4 variants where the old artifacts did not persist a common row-level uncertainty calculation; those differences remain descriptive / replicated-direction evidence rather than newly significant differences.

## Symmetric finding

No Phase-3/4 representation universally dominates every primary dimension.

- The standalone **D** transition-calibration component has the strongest standalone expected-points and state-quality profile in both validation and holdout among baseline/A1/A2/B/C/D, while **A2** is stronger on persistence calibration.
- Combining A2 with D improves the joint probability/points representation.
- **A2+B+D** has the lowest holdout points MAE of the three universal late combinations (30.3846), but its probability surface is the same A2+D surface and it is slightly worse than A2+C+D on validation points.
- **A2+C+D** improves persistence/state probability quality relative to A2+D and has validation points MAE 32.2099 and holdout 30.4038. Its holdout points difference versus A2+B+D is only about 0.019 points of MAE, far too small to treat as a scientific “winner” without paired uncertainty.
- The fixed **QB=A2+C+D / non-QB=A2+D** route is essentially tied with universal A2+C+D on aggregate validation (32.2106 MAE) and close on holdout (30.4272), while the original routing study documented removal of repeated young RB/WR/TE subgroup regressions. Under the symmetric rule this is a robustness tradeoff, not automatic routing authority.

## Authority interpretation

The Phase-3/4 evidence supports three durable conclusions for the broader audit:

1. continuous/hinged age information (A2) contributes real persistence information;
2. within-state magnitude / transition information (D, and later D0/D1 magnitude work) matters materially;
3. small aggregate differences among A2+B+D, A2+C+D and the fixed route are not enough by themselves to establish universal model authority.

The later D0/D1 and forecast-wide walk-forward studies remain the stronger common Y2/Y3 distributional coordinate for magnitude representation. Phase-3/4 therefore contributes serious candidate evidence but does not eliminate the need for the symmetric foundational audit.

No production Forecast, H3, or Intrinsic change is authorized.
