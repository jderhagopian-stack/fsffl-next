Date: 2026-09-27  
Authority: Research only; no production change.

## Scientific framing

These are **conditional-production components**, not independent whole-Forecast state architectures. They leave the routed state probabilities unchanged and therefore cannot earn whole-Forecast authority by themselves.

Under the symmetric rule, they are compared by the production quantities they actually change, with no preference for the historically selected component.

- State / persistence calibration was unchanged because the extension never changes probabilities.

Symmetric interpretation:
- **overall production:** directional benefit, statistically/cluster-uncertain rather than a broad victory;
- **extreme upper tail:** supported benefit;
- **lower-tail / non-persistence / role-loss:** no material supported harm;
- **RB Y3:** effectively tied / tiny adverse point estimate with interval spanning zero.

The correct scientific conclusion is therefore **conditional component with supported high-end value and uncertain broad average gain**, not “incumbent wins” or “challenger must clear a replacement threshold.”
